# Sam and the real moon

The davidup half of the hand-drawn film 4.0 acceptance film (`handdrawn/films/moon.js`). In it, sam walks
on beside a photograph of the full moon, turns to it and says goodnight. He is an alpha clip of
`handdrawn/films/sam-moon.js`, cut to the markers on a night pad and captioned by davidup in the film's hand.

    node examples/hdf-moon/build.mjs

It writes `assets/`, `composition.json` and `output/moon-real.mp4`, none of which are committed.

`full-moon.jpg`: "FullMoon2010.jpg" by Gregory H. Revera, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons, scaled to
1290 px wide. The composition carries the credit too: the asset is registered with `credit` and
`licence: "CC-BY-SA"`, and the editor's Library shows it under the thumbnail.
