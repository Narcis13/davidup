## Brief

- **For:** a 20 s teaser for Steep, a (made-up) loose-leaf tea subscription; the worked example of the new-video skill.
- **Audience and platform:** people who scroll fast; a site hero and a social post, 16:9.
- **Format:** 20 s, 16:9, 1920x1080, 30 fps
- **The one thing to remember:** good tea takes three minutes; take them.
- **Copy, in order:** "Everything is fast." / "Tea isn't." / "Steeped since 1755" (+ a hand-written "since 1755" note) / "Three minutes." with a 0:00 -> 3:00 timer / "Take them." / STEEP, "loose leaf, slowly", "steep.tea"
- **Must-haves:** the Met teapot, a real object with a history; a character (the fox) who takes the break; the wordmark on the end card.
- **Sound:** a calm bed made to measure (bed-calm-80: 80 bpm, a beat 0.75 s, a bar 3 s); pops on the hook's words, a whoosh into each act, ticks on the timer, a ding on the wordmark.
- **References:** refs/davidup-v1-1-showcase: taken for its structure (a fast hook that drops into calmer acts, an act every 3 to 4 s, a flash on the drop, the end card held 3 s). Not its look: Steep is warm and slow, not neon.

## Style card

- **Pacing:** 6 acts on 3 s bars: a busy hook (words on eighth notes), then acts of 3 to 4.5 s, the end card held 2.75 s. Arc: frantic -> sudden calm -> warm build -> a smile -> resolve.
- **Palette:** espresso #17110d (60), cream #f5ecdf (30), amber #e9a23b, terracotta #d4654a, sage #8fbfa8 (10).
- **Type:** Playfair Display Bold for statements (big, sentence case), DM Sans Medium for small print; one statement a shot.
- **Motion language:** the hook jitters (pops, shakes); everything after is slow: snap entrances, line reveals from a mask, drifts on holds. One transition family: terracotta/amber/cream bands wiping left. A single flash on the calm drop.
- **Hand-drawn layer (chalkboard look, cream and amber chalk):** a circle round the teapot with a written callout "since 1755", an underline under "Three minutes.", a starburst round the cup when the fox arrives, an underline under the wordmark.
- **Texture and depth:** kraft paper grain over everything (overlay, low), warm aurora blobs behind, soft drop shadows under the cut-outs, a slow push-in on every hold.
